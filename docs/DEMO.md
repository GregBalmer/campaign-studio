# Demo video script (2–3 minutes)

The video has one job: show the required pipeline working, in the assignment's order, with a person approving the decisions that matter. Record at 1440×900, the browser full-screen. Target: about 2:45.

**Before recording:** start the app (`python3 start.py`) and sign in. Click **Restart demo** beside the campaign name. Open **Models** and check **OpenAI** is selected for images and translation. Have the README open on GitHub in another tab.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | README on GitHub | "This is Campaign Studio. One brief in, every social ad out, in every size and market, with a person approving the decisions that matter." |
| 0:12 | Brief | "It's running on this computer: one command, or a double-click, sets it up. It opens on a sample: two products, two markets, one message. One product has a photo, so we'll reuse it. The other doesn't, so we'll generate it. The panel on the right says what we'll get: twelve ads." |
| 0:37 | Click **Create key visuals** | "Nothing gets resized or translated yet. First, one key visual per product, for a person to approve." |
| 0:47 | Key visual: photo | "This is the client's photo. I fit the box around the product; every size is cropped around it. The outlines show where the Story and Banner crops land, and the previews update live." Approve. |
| 1:07 | Key visual: generated | "This one was generated by OpenAI from the brief in about ten seconds. If it's wrong, **Try another** costs one image, not twelve ads." Fit the box. Approve. |
| 1:27 | Message | "The line is adapted for Japan, not just translated, and here's what it means in English, so I can sign off without reading Japanese." Type a restricted word to show the flag, then undo. "Legal's restricted words are caught before anyone approves." Approve both. |
| 1:52 | Review | "Twelve ads. Every one has been checked for legibility, logo, brand colors, restricted words, and whether the product is cropped or covered. Problems are marked on the ad itself." Open the flagged one. "It says what's wrong and how to fix it." |
| 2:17 | **Approve all that pass**, **Download**, then **Show folder** | "Approve, download. And here's the folder: by product, then by size, with a report of who approved what and when." |
| 2:35 | Models panel | "Images and translation are chosen separately: free demo images, OpenAI, or Adobe Firefly with the client's own credentials. The Firefly adapter is written to Adobe's documentation and tested against a stand-in server, not yet on a live account." |

**If anything fails on camera:** the app explains the error in plain words. That's worth keeping in the take.
